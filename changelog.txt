"Forgotten Depths" Major Update (6.0.0) (Beta-A):

Main Additions:
[+] Tungsten Rework:
    - Tungsten ore is less common, and now it can also be obtained from piglins and bastions (brute piglins drop the most tungsten, but piglins are far more common, and bastions sometimes have tungsten blocks in replacement of gold blocks)
    - Reinforced templates are now used to reinforce any item (That has durability), increasing their durability by 50% (80% if it's an armor piece). additionally:
        - Reinforced shields can't be disarmed
        - Reinforced items become fire-resistant
    - Coating templates are used to coat any item, increasing their attributes (E.g. Damage) by 50%, and decreasing their durability by 30% (15% if it's an armor piece). additionally:
        - Coated armor pieces gain -40% Burning Time
        - Coated tools gain +2 mining efficiency (if it already has mining efficiency from another source, then only the +50% effect applies)
        - Coated weapons gain +0.5 knockback (if it already has knockback from another source, then only the +50% effect applies)
        - Coated shields deflect 60% of the received damage
    - You can use Tungsten to barter with piglins for better drops, including a chance to obtain reinforced/coating templates (or items with 'similar' value)
    - Brute Piglins have a 10% chance to spawn with a Tungsten Hogaxe instead of a Golden Axe (yes they deal more damage with it)
